## 3. Patterns and user needs

- **Table stakes:** all six London airports; flight monitoring; meet and greet; E/S/V-Class; 24/7 availability; "reply within the hour".
- **Premium look has converged:** navy or black with gold and a display serif (Cinzel, Cormorant, Fraunces). A calmer, distinct identity stands out.
- **Most "local" rankers aren't local:** Laindon, Welwyn, national operators. The firms that really are local (Paul's, Billy, Capital) have the weakest websites.
- **Licensing is vague almost everywhere.** Only Lawlor names its council.
- **Customers and their needs (inferred from services, testimonials and search behaviour):**
  - Local households booking early flights and late returns, who want certainty the car will be there.
  - PAs and bureaux booking speakers and executives, who want reliability and discretion; T&L's own clients are speaker bureaux.
  - Groups needing seats and luggage space.
  - Older clients and hospital visits, who need legible type and a phone number that is always visible.
- **Search intent:**
  - "chauffeur / executive car + Theydon Bois / Loughton / Epping / Chigwell"
  - "Stansted / Heathrow transfer from Theydon Bois"
  - postcode modifiers (IG10, IG7, IG9, CM16)
  - occasions (weddings, race days, theatre)
  - cruise ports
  - minibus / 8-seater
